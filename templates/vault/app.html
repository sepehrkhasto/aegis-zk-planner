{% comment %} v6.4 — four destinations and one action. This bar used to carry five equal tabs, one of which was Reports: a screen almost nobody opens on a phone, sitting in the most valuable real estate the interface has. Testers said the app was cluttered and that they could not tell where to start. What stays is what someone reaches for on a phone: today, the calendar, the task list. Everything else moves behind "More", which is a destination in its own right rather than a drawer of leftovers. Adding is not a tab — it is the thing you came to do — so it sits in the middle as a raised button, the pattern every phone user already knows without being taught. {% endcomment %}
